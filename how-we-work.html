HOW WE WORK · BLENDER DIGITAL

You run the restaurant.
We help your delivery business work harder for you.

Running a restaurant leaves little time to investigate a sales dip, question an advertising recommendation, or figure out why a promotion is generating orders without leaving enough money behind.

That’s where we come in. Blender Digital becomes an extension of your team—managing the details, identifying opportunities, and helping you make informed decisions about your online ordering channels.

We work in focused four- or six-month engagements, with a clear goal: leave your delivery business stronger and give you the confidence to run it yourself.

A PARTNER IN YOUR CORNER

Small restaurants deserve hands-on support.

You don’t need dozens of locations or a dedicated marketing department to work with us.

Independent restaurants and family-run shops are at the heart of what we do. We’ve also worked with brands with more than 65 locations, so we understand how needs change as a business grows. We adapt our approach to your restaurant, your resources, and the support your team actually needs.

Sometimes that means improving an established delivery business. Sometimes it means helping you get started.

GETTING STARTED

Getting started shouldn’t become another job.

We guide you through the access and information we need, review your existing setup, and prioritize the first changes together.

We look at your storefronts, menus, pricing, reviews, campaigns, and available performance history. We also ask about the things a dashboard can’t tell us: kitchen capacity, food costs, staffing constraints, and what a good outcome looks like for you.

You bring the knowledge of your restaurant. We bring the experience to turn it into a practical delivery plan.

THE WORK BETWEEN THE REPORTS

An extra pair of eyes on the details that matter.

Our work continues between reports and strategy calls. We monitor the signals that can affect your sales, customer experience, and money coming back to the restaurant.

Errors and refund requests
Review charges, investigate what happened, and coordinate disputes or operational follow-up where appropriate.
Customer reviews
Respond thoughtfully, flag feedback that needs attention, and share the positive comments your team deserves to see.
Sales trends
Investigate unusual changes in orders, average order value, and customer activity.
Campaign performance
Check spending, redemptions, returns, and unexpected behavior.
Platform issues
Work through questions and follow-ups with support teams and account representatives.

When something needs your input, we explain what we found, what we recommend, and what decision is needed.

A FOCUSED FOUR- TO SIX-MONTH ENGAGEMENT

Build the foundation. Test carefully. Improve consistently.

Every restaurant starts somewhere different. Our process follows the evidence rather than a fixed campaign template.

  1. Understand the starting point

    We establish a baseline and identify the most important constraints. A restaurant with weak visibility needs a different approach from one receiving plenty of orders at an unsustainable discount.

  2. Find what earns its place

    We test relevant offers and advertising budgets, examine customer response, and adjust. We reduce or stop activity that isn’t working and carefully expand what is.

    Customer segmentation and time-specific campaigns are useful when they solve a real problem. We don’t add complexity simply because a platform offers it.

  3. Strengthen the economics

    As we learn, we refine who receives an offer, how much is spent, and where further investment makes sense. Growth may be the early priority; improving what you retain from those sales may become the next.

  4. Prepare you to take over

    We explain the settings, spending decisions, and performance signals that matter. The aim is a manageable setup and a clear understanding of when to maintain, adjust, or stop an activity.

PROFITABILITY, WITH CONTEXT

More sales should mean more money left for you.

Explore the ROI calculator →

A sales increase is only part of the story. We want to understand what it took to generate it—and whether you’re better off after paying for it.

That means looking beyond campaign revenue and reported return on ad spend. We consider platform fees, restaurant-funded discounts, advertising, refunds, and the food, packaging, and other relevant costs you share with us.

Our management fee belongs in that calculation, too.

Our goal is for the additional channel profit to cover our service and leave more money in your business. Where cost information is incomplete, we make the assumptions clear. A platform payout is a useful measure, but it isn’t the same thing as profit.

YOUR RESTAURANT’S INTERESTS FIRST

Someone on your side of the platform conversation.

Platform representatives can provide useful support, funding opportunities, and account insights. We help you assess their recommendations against your restaurant’s goals and economics.

Before increasing spend or launching an offer, we ask practical questions: What will the restaurant fund? Who will receive the promotion? What would make it worthwhile? How will we know when to adjust?

We handle follow-ups and help translate recommendations into decisions you can feel comfortable making.
