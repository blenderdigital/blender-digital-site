Chrome Extension Privacy Policy

Payout Ratio for Delivery Apps

This policy explains how Blender Digital handles information when you use the Payout Ratio for Delivery Apps Chrome extension.

What the extension does

The extension adds a payout-ratio column to supported DoorDash Merchant Portal and Uber Eats Manager monthly statement tables.

It reads the gross sales or earnings value and the net total value already visible in each statement row, then calculates the payout ratio by dividing net total by gross sales.

How information is processed

All calculations occur locally within your browser and are used only to display the payout ratio on the same webpage.

Information we do not collect

The extension does not collect, store, transmit, sell, share, or retain:

  • Restaurant statement or financial data
  • Personally identifiable information
  • Login credentials or authentication information
  • Browsing history or activity outside the supported statement pages
  • Cookies or location information

Analytics, advertising, and remote code

The extension does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party data-processing services. It does not download or execute remotely hosted code.

Website access

The extension is designed to operate only on the supported DoorDash Merchant Portal and Uber Eats Manager statement pages declared in its Chrome extension package. It does not require access to unrelated websites.

Data sharing and sale

Because the extension does not collect or transmit user data, Blender Digital does not sell or share extension user data with third parties.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. Any material changes will be reflected on this page and, where applicable, in the Chrome Web Store disclosures.
